THE PLAYER
  js-dos 8.5.3 (GPL-2.0) — https://js-dos.com
  DOSBox (GPL-2.0) via its wdosbox WebAssembly build.
  Vendored, self-hosted, minimal set:
    js-dos.js, js-dos.css
    emulators/emulators.js, wdosbox.js, wdosbox.wasm,
    wlibzip.js, wlibzip.wasm
  No js-dos CDN is contacted at runtime; the player runs entirely
  from this site, in the visitor's own tab.

SAVES
  The player's saves live in the visitor's own browser storage
  (js-dos local persistence). The site receives nothing.
